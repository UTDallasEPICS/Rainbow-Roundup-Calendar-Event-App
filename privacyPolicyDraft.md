# Rainbow Roundup Privacy Policy Draft
This is a draft made by Samuel Ma. This has not necessarily been approved by a certified law entity. 
**Last Updated November 14th, 2026.**
## Summary
With this privacy policy, (hereby named "the Policy"), we want to let you know how Rainbow Roundup (hereby named "Rainbow Roundup" or "us") promise to take care of the personal information you provide us with. 

We only collect, use, store and transfer (“process”) your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection legislation (hereby named "applicable law"), and all of it is done with your explicit consent. You have a right to withdraw consent at any time, and ask us to remove your data at any time. The application should also enable you to remove all your data yourself, except email, which is necessary for application usage. 

If you have any questions about the personal information we collect, how we use your data and how we transfer your data to fulfil our purposes as stated herein, please contact us at email: `info@rrup.org`. Below, you will find more details about how we use personal information.

## Rainbow Roundup
Rainbow Roundup is a nonprofit company behind events that matter to LGBT families around Texas. We and Non-Profit Technical Support are responsible for protecting your data that you voluntarily enter.

## Non-Profit Technical Support
Non-Profit Technical Support (pronounced "endpoints," hereby referred to as NPTS) hosts the domain and web app that Rainbow Roundup uses. If you have further questions about how the application works under the hood, please email `info@npts.tech` or `samuel.ma@npts.tech`

## What We Promise
We promise to keep your data safe. We commit to giving you the right to access and delete your data at any time. We nor NPTS will NEVER share data with third parties (e.g. any entity that does not work for Rainbow Roundup nor NPTS). 

## What is Personal Information?
Personal information can be any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. In other words, it can be any information that could allow us to identify you both directly and indirectly (like for example your name, email address). In this Policy, we will use the word “data” to refer to personal information.

## Legal Grounds for Collecting your Data
We collect data ONLY: 
* When you consent to it by submitting data. 
* To fulfill a contract we have with you (for example, when you have purchased something through our storefront).
We process data:
* When it's in our legitimate interest (for example, data analytics, or shipping orders, or merchandise pickup at events)

## When do we Collect your Data?
We collect your data ONLY when you fill it out yourself. The only piece of data that we strictly require from you to fully use our services is your email for authentication purposes. Every other piece of data is completely voluntary. 

## What Data do we Collect?
As of writing, we collect your:
* First Name
* Last Name
* Email (required)
* Address
* Phone Number
* How many children are in your household and their age ranges.

## Why do we Process your Data?
We want to keep our operations at the highest quality possible, and by looking at the big picture of data (for example, if there are a lot of 5 year old kids coming to certain kinds of events, we'd want to include more activities in the future for those events)

## Who do we Share your Information with?
We share your information with no one except NPTS, who are the maintainers of the application to begin with (in layman's terms, they have direct access to data by default). 

## Can Children use our Services?
Yes, though we recommend your parent own the family account. 

## Security Measures to Protect your Data
We maintain physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to help guard your data in compliance with applicable privacy regulations. If transactions are offered on the Services, transaction information is transmitted to and from the Service in encrypted form using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS used to be known as SSL) connections to help protect such information from interception. We strictly restrict authorized access to your data only to those persons who have a legitimate purpose to access it. We also have security measures in place to protect the loss, misuse and alteration of the data we process. The only people that see your data are Admins, who work for Rainbow Roundup, and NPTS, who maintain the application. 

## If you Choose not to Share your Data with us
We are completely happy even if you don't want to share any data (barring email, due to the application not working if you don't provide your email)

## How Long will we Retain your Data?
We will keep your data as long as you use our services, but we will delete accounts after three (3) years of not having used our services at all. **We will also delete data upon request and you are able to delete your data yourself through the application.**

## Right to be Forgotten
You are entitled to request that we delete data related to you. We will delete your data upon request. 

## How do I get a Copy of my Data, Delete it, or Enforce Other Rights to it?
Just drop us an email. Please note that if we cannot identify and guarantee who you are, we might ask you to provide us with additional information. Please note that we will not delete any data if we are unable to verify that you are their rightful owner. We will however always use our best effort to help you out. 

## Changes in the Privacy Policy
This Policy may change from time to time. Any changes to this Policy will be posted on this page, and if the changes are significant, we will provide you with a more noticeable notice, like for example by email.
